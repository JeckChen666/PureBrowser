Recipe: tools/fixtures/generate_hls_fixture.sh
Short fixture: 8 seconds, 320x180, 24 fps, H.264 High level 1.2, two B-frames,
closed 48-frame GOP, 48 kHz AAC-LC mono; four separate two-second MPEG-TS segments
on a single continuous encode/mux timestamp timeline. Optional generator output
may instead be 1801 seconds with mono or stereo AAC and a final one-second segment.
No FFmpeg dependency is needed to serve or consume the already generated media.
