## 1. What is, and is not, established

- **Eight distinct works**, with **two YouTube candidates and six non-YouTube candidates across three other playback platforms**. This is a candidate inventory, not eight fully frozen/approved corpus rows.
- Platform groups are **YouTube**, **Blender PeerTube**, **Wikimedia Commons**, and **Internet Archive**. The two Blender Studio project pages checked use YouTube embeds: they supply authorship/licensing evidence only and **do not add a fifth service**. Object-storage URLs, alternative resolutions, and subdomains are not additional services.
- All eight have primary-service work-identity metadata and explicit CC declarations. **All eight selected authorization-basis versions are now fixed.** The October 4 follow-up corroborated primary-author full-movie grants for Spring, Elephants Dream, Cosmos Laundromat and Caminandes 2. Service/uploader labels are preserved separately, not silently rewritten as the author grants.
- **The four pending primary-author corroborations are closed at the underlying-work / metadata-identity level.** Their grants cover the identified **unchanged movies**, not unrelated uploads, added third-party content, or arbitrary soundtrack replacements. Selected mirror bytes/credits have not been compared. No strict corpus freeze, anonymous-playback validation or paired app baseline is established; this is not eight fully inspected renditions.
- All works have related Blender/open-movie provenance. This tests different playback platforms, **not independent creators or every type of production website**.
- No credentials, login, protected-media bypass, media download, AVD/device run, private report/file inspection, or source implementation work was performed. Existing public documentation was read only for scope and inherited Sintel context.
- **No new app success/failure result exists.** All eight new paired `v0.1.4`/`v0.1.5` baselines are `NOT_TESTED_BY_SIDECAR`; device evidence is null. A CC grant does not establish platform-policy compliance, anonymous availability, an executable plan, or a usable saved output.

### Evidence terminology

**Verified metadata** means a primary HTML/JSON source was read and contained the recorded field/declaration. It does not mean the player was exercised or the media bytes were fetched. **Playback page** is the public watch/file/item entry associated with that identity; actual anonymous playback remains pending. YouTube oEmbed confirms the title, publisher, and embed association but not anonymous watch-page accessibility. Archive playback entries use the confirmed item identifiers; `/details` HTML/player rendering was not fetched.

`knownNormalDirectLinks` is **empty for every row**: no media endpoint was requested, even with HEAD. PeerTube has separately recorded **published media declarations**: exact URLs copied from HTTP-200 public JSON, not synthesized or claimed byte/HTTP verified. Archive has verified filenames only, **not constructed `/download/...` URLs**. Commons original-media targets were not followed.

The initial inventory used **19 direct HTTP metadata attempts plus five web primary-page open attempts** (24 total, including repeats/failures). The separately requested **October 4 author-corroboration follow-up used 16 direct metadata attempts plus one web primary-page open attempt (17 total)**, then stopped. Discovery searches supplied pointers only. Follow-up fetches had a 15-second timeout and 400 KB text/JSON cap; no media bytes were requested. Counts are per batch, not a claim that cumulative research stayed at 20 requests. Neither batch establishes a frozen/device-tested corpus.
